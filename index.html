From Information Kiosks to Spatial AI

The Place Becomes the Interface.

For decades, information kiosks have helped visitors understand large physical environments.

They tell us:

  • You are here.
  • Here is what surrounds you.
  • Here is where you need to go.

Location-aware AI transforms that interaction.

Instead of finding the kiosk, the intelligence comes with you.

A MyGeoVerse-powered AI Guide can understand where the visitor is, connect that location with information about the surrounding environment, answer questions naturally, and help the visitor navigate and explore.

The Museum That Talks Back

From Audio Guide to AI Guide

Traditional museum audio guides tell every visitor a predetermined story.

A location-aware AI Guide can turn the museum into a conversation.

As visitors move through the museum, the AI Guide can understand their location and connect them with the stories, objects, people and moments associated with that part of the museum.

Visitors can ask:

What am I looking at? Why is this important? Who used this? What happened here? Tell me the story behind this object. Can you explain this to my child? Tell me more.

Location-Aware

The AI Guide understands where the visitor is within the museum and connects the conversation with the surrounding exhibits.

Conversational AI

Visitors ask questions naturally and explore according to their own curiosity.

Spatial Storytelling

Artifacts, photographs, people, places and historic moments can become connected through spatially anchored digital content.

Multilingual

The same museum can communicate with visitors in multiple languages through a conversational interface.

Personalized Experience

The conversation can adapt to the visitor's questions, interests and available time.

Navigation

Visitors can be guided toward exhibits, museum sections and Points of Interest.

AI Avatar

The guide can take the form of a character, historical figure, mascot or custom 3D avatar.

Audio-First

Not every experience requires AR graphics. A powerful AI Guide experience can be almost entirely conversational and audio-based. The solution is Spatial AI, not simply AR.
